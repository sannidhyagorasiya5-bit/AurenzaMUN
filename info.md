## Design System (Observed)

**Color**

- Background: near-black with warm brown and orange radial gradients
- Primary accent: orange (approx. #E8622A), used for CTAs, second-line headlines, School track
- Secondary accent: green, used for Committee and Secretariat headings, College track
- Tertiary accent: purple, reserved for the IP Committee track
- Body text: light grey on dark

**Typography**

- Heavy geometric sans-serif for display headings
- All-caps headlines, two-line stacked treatment with color split between lines
- Wide letter-spacing on eyebrow labels and navigation

**Components**

- Pill-shaped badges for metadata and role labels
- Rounded cards with subtle glass/translucent surfaces
- Dashed borders indicating disabled or pending states
- Numbered step blocks with circular index markers
- Floating overlay cards on hero imagery
